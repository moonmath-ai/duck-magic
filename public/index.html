HOW TO PLAY · DUCK MAGIC

Survive the duck.

The duck casts first. Outsmart its spells and knock it out before you run out of health.

  1. 01
    Read its spell.

    The duck tells you what's coming. An unblocked attack costs you 10 HP.

  2. 02
    Invent a defense.

    Write what you do and why it works. A clever reversal can hurt the duck.

  3. 03
    Watch your magic.

    Spells use magic. You recover 2 magic after each turn, but HP doesn't refill. The duck notices repeated tactics and may evade the return path.
